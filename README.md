# 🧠 Distributed and Pervasive Intelligence (DIPI) Lab

The Distributed and Pervasive Intelligence (DIPI) Lab is a research group at the [University of Modena and Reggio Emilia](https://www.unimore.it), Italy and in particular associated to the Department of Sciences and Methods for Engineering (DISMI).

## 🔬 Research Areas

Our research focuses on:

- 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
- 📊 Machine Learning
- 🌐 Distributed and Pervasive Computing
- 🔌 Internet of Things (IoT) & Industrial IoT (IIoT)
- Agents & Multi-Agent Systems
- 👥 Digital Twin

## 🎯 Application Domains

We work on applications in:
- 🏭 Industry 4.0
- 🚗 Transportation and Mobility
- 🔗 Networking
